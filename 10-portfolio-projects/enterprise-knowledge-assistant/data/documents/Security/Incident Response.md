# Security Incident Response — Sample

## What counts as an incident
Examples include a suspected credential leak, unauthorized data access, malware alert, suspicious privilege change, or unexpected exposure of sensitive information.

## Initial response
1. Report the concern through the designated security channel immediately.
2. Include when it was noticed, affected system, observed behavior, and relevant event or request IDs.
3. Preserve evidence. Do not delete logs or make unauthorized changes.
4. Follow the incident lead's instructions for containment.
5. Rotate or revoke potentially exposed credentials through approved procedures.
6. Document actions and timestamps in the incident record.

## After recovery
Confirm service restoration, identify follow-up owners, and document lessons learned. Do not share sensitive incident details outside the response group.

## Note
This is fictional educational material, not a substitute for an approved incident-response plan.
