# Finance Controls — Sample Reference

## Expense submissions
Submit expenses with a business purpose, date, category, amount, and itemized receipt where required. Follow the organization’s current expense tool and approval matrix.

## Approval
Expenses outside a team’s pre-approved limit must be reviewed by the designated manager before reimbursement. A submitter must not approve their own expense.

## Procurement
Purchases should use an approved supplier and documented business need. Follow the purchase-order process before committing funds. Split transactions must not be used to avoid approval thresholds.

## Note
The sample policy is fictional. Real organizations must use their official finance policies and approval matrix.
