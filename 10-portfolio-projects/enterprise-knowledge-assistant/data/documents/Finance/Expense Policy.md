# Expense Policy — Sample

## Submitting an expense
Provide a business purpose, expense date, category, amount, and itemized receipt where required. Submit expenses through the approved finance workflow.

## Approval
Expenses above the employee's delegated limit require manager approval before reimbursement. Employees cannot approve their own expenses. If a purchase is unusual or outside policy, ask Finance before committing funds.

## Timelines
Submit claims within the published reimbursement window. If a receipt is missing, use the finance team's documented exception process rather than creating substitute evidence.

## Note
Thresholds and timelines in a real company depend on its formal expense policy. This sample is fictional and intentionally has no real monetary limits.
