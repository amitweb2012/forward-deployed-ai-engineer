# Procurement Policy — Sample

## Before purchase
Document the business need, estimated total cost, delivery expectations, and data or security considerations. Prefer approved suppliers and follow the purchase-order process.

## Reviews
Obtain the required budget-owner approval before committing company funds. Purchases involving software, sensitive data, or access to internal systems should receive the required security and privacy reviews.

## Vendor onboarding
Use the approved vendor onboarding process. Do not share confidential company information with a prospective supplier before the appropriate agreement and approval are in place.

## Integrity
Do not split purchases to bypass approval rules, accept undisclosed benefits from suppliers, or place orders on behalf of another person without authorization.

## Note
This is fictional educational sample content and does not specify real approval limits.
